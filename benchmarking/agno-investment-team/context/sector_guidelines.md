# Sector Allocation Guidelines

## Maximum Sector Exposure

- Technology: 40%
- Healthcare: 25%
- Financials: 20%
- Any other individual sector: 15%

## Approved Sectors

- Technology
- Healthcare
- Consumer Discretionary
- Consumer Staples
- Financials
- Energy
- Industrials
- Communication Services
- Utilities

Sector limits must be respected at all times.
No sector may exceed its maximum allocation after a new position is added.
