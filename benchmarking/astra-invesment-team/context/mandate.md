# Investment Mandate

## Fund Overview

- Fund Size: $10,000,000
- Asset Class: US-listed large-cap public equities only
- Benchmark: S&P 500
- Investment Horizon: 12–18 months

## Eligibility Criteria

- Minimum market cap: $10B
- Share price must be above $5
- Must be listed on major US exchanges (NYSE, NASDAQ)
- No ADRs
- No OTC securities
- No SPACs or blank check companies

## Capital Constraints

- Maximum 15 positions at any time
- Must maintain minimum 5% cash reserve ($500,000)
- No leverage
- No short selling
- No derivatives
