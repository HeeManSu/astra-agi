# Investment Process

## Evaluation Pipeline

1. Market Assessment
   - Macro environment
   - Liquidity regime
   - Sector tailwinds/headwinds

2. Fundamental Analysis
   - Revenue growth
   - Profitability
   - Cash flow strength
   - Balance sheet quality
   - Competitive positioning

3. Valuation Analysis
   - Intrinsic value estimation
   - Peer comparison
   - Margin of safety

4. Technical Analysis
   - Trend structure
   - Momentum
   - Entry timing quality

5. Risk Assessment
   - Downside scenarios
   - Volatility profile
   - Mandate compliance
   - Position sizing constraints

6. Investment Memo
   - Structured documentation of findings

7. Committee Decision
   - Final vote: BUY / HOLD / PASS
   - Dollar allocation
   - Time horizon
   - Review trigger
