# Risk Policy

## Position Limits

- Maximum single position: 30% of fund ($3,000,000)
- Minimum position size: 2% of fund ($200,000)

## Beta-Based Adjustments

- If stock beta > 1.5 → maximum allocation capped at 15%

## Position Sizing Categories

- High Conviction: 15–25%
- Standard: 5–15%
- Exploratory: 2–5%

## Portfolio-Level Risk

- Maximum portfolio beta: 1.5
- Maximum drawdown tolerance: 20%
- Correlation limit: No two positions may exceed 0.85 correlation

## Risk Controls

- Quarterly full review
- Monthly risk monitoring
- Trim position if it exceeds 35% due to appreciation
- Exit position if it declines 25% from entry price
