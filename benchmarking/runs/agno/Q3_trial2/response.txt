**Comparable Multiples:**
*   Trailing P/E: 46.08
*   Forward P/E: 19.71
*   PEG Ratio: 0.75
*   EV/EBITDA: 40.71
*   Price/Book: 34.81

NVDA's PEG ratio of 0.75 suggests that the stock may be undervalued relative to its earnings growth. However, the high trailing P/E, EV/EBITDA, and Price/Book multiples indicate that the market has high growth expectations already priced in. The overall assessment from the comparable multiples is "Fairly valued," which contrasts with the DCF.

**Fair Value & Margin of Safety:**
Given the discrepancy, a blended approach is necessary. The DCF, even with aggressive growth assumptions, yields a significantly lower intrinsic value. The comparable multiples suggest fair valuation, but this is heavily influenced by the PEG ratio, which can be volatile.

*   Bear Case Fair Value: $59.39
*   Base Case Fair Value: $124.15 (average of DCF base case and current price implied by multiples)
*   Bull Case Fair Value: $151.57

With a current price of $225.32 and a Base Case Fair Value of $124.15, the Margin of Safety is -81.5%, and the Upside Potential is -44.9%. The verdict is **Overvalued**, with a negative margin of safety.

**Valuation Risks:**
High growth expectations are priced in, making the stock sensitive to any slowdown. The DCF is highly sensitive to the discount rate. The competitive landscape, inflationary pressures, and the debt-to-equity ratio (despite significant cash) are also risk factors.

**Final Valuation Verdict:** Based on the comprehensive valuation analysis, NVDA appears **Overvalued**. The current market price seems to have fully priced in, if not exceeded, the company's strong growth prospects and profitability. Investors should exercise caution at the current valuation.
