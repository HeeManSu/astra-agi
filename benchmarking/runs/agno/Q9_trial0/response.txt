## TSLA Research Packet

### 1. MACRO ANALYSIS

The current macro environment presents a mixed but generally supportive picture for equities, leading to a **risk-on** environment. Monetary policy is neutral with stable rates (Fed Funds Rate at 3.64%) and a positive 10Y-2Y yield spread of 0.47%, indicating no immediate recession concerns and stable longer-term growth expectations. Inflation is rising (Headline CPI at 3.95% YoY), but core inflation remains contained (2.99% YoY). GDP growth is rising at 0.49% QoQ, and the unemployment rate is moderate at 4.3%, suggesting a growing economy. Liquidity conditions are easing, as evidenced by a negative Chicago Fed NFCI of -0.524 and tight corporate credit spreads (0.76%). Overall, the macro environment is conducive to equity market performance.

### 2. FINANCIAL ANALYSIS

Tesla demonstrates strong financial fundamentals in several key areas. The company exhibits solid revenue growth (15.8% YoY) with accelerating quarterly earnings growth (16.6% YoY), indicating continued expansion. Cash flow generation is robust, with $16.53 billion in operating cash flow and $5.25 billion in free cash flow, highlighting the strength of its core business. Tesla's balance sheet is exceptionally strong, boasting $44.74 billion in cash against $15.89 billion in total debt, resulting in a substantial net cash position of $28.85 billion. The debt-to-equity ratio is low (18.738%), and the current ratio is healthy (2.043), signifying excellent liquidity and minimal reliance on debt.

However, there are areas of concern. Tesla's profitability margins are relatively modest, with a gross margin of 19.1%, operating margin of 4.2%, and net margin of 3.9%. Return on equity (4.9%) and return on assets (2.2%) are also on the lower side. Furthermore, the high PEG ratio of 6.1 suggests that the stock might be overvalued relative to its expected earnings growth, implying that significant future growth is already priced in.

### 3. VALUATION ANALYSIS

Based on both Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) and comparable multiples analysis, TSLA appears significantly overvalued at its current price of $422.24.

**DCF Valuation:** Even with optimistic assumptions reflecting the risk-on environment and strong financial position (9.0% WACC, 18.0% FCF growth for 7 years, 4.0% terminal growth), the intrinsic value per share ranges from a Bear Case of $38.88 to a Bull Case of $103.68. This is substantially lower than the current market price, resulting in a negative margin of safety.

**Comparable Multiples:** TSLA's multiples are exceptionally high across the board: Trailing P/E of 383.85x, Forward P/E of 167.95x, PEG Ratio of 6.1x, EV/EBITDA of 140.41x, Price/Book of 19.28x, and Price/Sales of 16.2x. These figures indicate that the market has priced in extremely aggressive future growth, far exceeding that of traditional automotive and most technology peers.

**Valuation Risks:** The primary risks include the unsustainability of current growth expectations, increasing competition in the EV and technology sectors, and the relatively modest profitability margins compared to the high valuation. Any slowdown in growth or failure to significantly improve profitability could lead to a substantial re-rating of the stock. The valuation verdict is a **PASS**, indicating that the stock is not considered a good value at its current price.
