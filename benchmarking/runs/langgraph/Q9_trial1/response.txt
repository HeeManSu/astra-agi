## 2. FINANCIAL ANALYSIS

### 1. Revenue & Growth Analysis

Tesla (TSLA) demonstrates solid revenue growth of 15.8% and earnings growth of 8.3%. Quarterly earnings growth is even stronger at 16.6%, indicating an accelerating trend. The forward EPS of $2.51 suggests continued earnings expansion.

Return on Equity (ROE) is 4.9%, and Return on Assets (ROA) is 2.2%. Tesla exhibits strong cash flow generation. Operating cash flow is robust at $16.53 billion, and free cash flow is a healthy $5.25 billion. This indicates the company's ability to generate substantial cash from its core operations and fund its investments.

### 4. Balance Sheet Quality

The balance sheet appears strong. TSLA holds a significant total cash position of $44.74 billion against total debt of $15.89 billion, resulting in a net cash position of $28.85 billion. The debt-to-equity ratio is 18.738, which is manageable, and the current ratio of 2.043 indicates good short-term liquidity.

### 5. Earnings Stability

With a trailing EPS of $1.1 and a forward EPS of $2.51, Tesla shows a positive outlook for earnings. The consistent revenue and earnings growth, coupled with strong quarterly earnings growth, suggest a degree of earnings stability and future potential. However, the PEG ratio of 6.1 is quite high, implying that the stock may be overvalued relative to its earnings growth.

### 6. Financial Risk Factors

While the balance sheet is strong with ample cash and manageable debt, the relatively low profitability margins and ROE/ROA could be a concern if growth slows. The high PEG ratio also suggests that the market has high expectations for future growth, and any miss could lead to significant price corrections.
